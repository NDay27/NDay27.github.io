How we build a home

The same house, start to finish. This is what you'll see in your Friday updates.

{{STAGE_IMGS}}

Weeks 1 to 3

  1. Weeks 1 to 3

    Footings and foundation

    We stake the corners with you, dig, pour footings and set the foundation walls. Nothing goes up until the base is square to within a quarter inch.

  2. Weeks 4 to 6

    Floor deck and first walls

    Joists, subfloor, then walls at 16 inches on center with real headers over every opening. You can walk your rooms by week six.

  3. Weeks 7 to 9

    Second floor and roof framing

    The second floor goes up, then rafters and the ridge. This is the week most families come out for pictures.

  4. Weeks 10 to 12

    Dried in

    Sheathing, house wrap, roof deck and windows. Once it's dried in, weather stops being a schedule problem.

  5. Weeks 13 to 18

    Siding, roof and porch

    Board-and-batten, standing-seam metal and the timber porch, while the inside crews run wiring, plumbing and drywall.

  6. Weeks 19 to 34

    Finishes, then the keys

    Cabinets, floors, paint, landscaping and the final walkthrough. We fix the punch list before you sign off, not after.

Questions people ask first

Do you build on my lot, or do I need to buy one of yours?{{PLUS}}

Either. Most of our homes go on land the family already owns. If you're still looking, we'll walk lots with you before you buy and tell you what the site will cost to build on.

Can I bring my own plans?{{PLUS}}

Yes. We price your architect's plans as drawn, or our designer can draw a plan with you. Either way you get one fixed price before anything starts.

What happens if I want to change something mid-build?{{PLUS}}

You get a written change order with the price and any schedule impact. Nothing changes until you sign it, so the final bill never surprises you.

How do payments work?{{PLUS}}

10% at signing, then draws tied to finished stages: foundation, framing, dried in, drywall and final. Most families pay through their construction loan.

How far do you travel?{{PLUS}}

About 30 miles from Morton: Peoria, East Peoria, Washington, Pekin, Tremont, Eureka, Germantown Hills, Metamora and the towns in between.
