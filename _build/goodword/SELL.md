# Good Word: the one-minute phone script

Price: $39 a month. First 14 days free. No contract. Setup is done for them the same day.
With a website plan: add it for $29 a month.

## Before you dial (20 seconds)

Look up their Google listing. Write down two numbers: their review count, and the review count of the busiest competitor in town. Open nday27.github.io/goodword/setup and make their preview link.

## The script (about 60 seconds)

**Open (10 sec)**
"Hi, is this [owner]? This is Nolan Day, I run a small web studio in Morton, Illinois. Quick one, I'm not selling a website today. Got one minute?"

**The reason (15 sec)**
"I looked you up on Google. You've got [their number] reviews, and [competitor] has [bigger number]. Your reviews are good, there just aren't many. That's usually because nobody has time to ask."

**The offer (20 sec)**
"I made a little app that fixes that. At the end of a job you type the customer's first name, tap once, and it texts them your Google review link from your own phone. It remembers who you asked and reminds them once. I already set one up with [business name] on it."

**The ask (15 sec)**
"Can I text you the link so you can try it right now? It's free for two weeks. If you like it, it's 39 dollars a month, no contract. What's the best cell number?"

Then text them the preview link from the setup page while you are still on the phone, and stay on until they open it.

## If they say

- **"I already ask people."** "Good, then this just makes it faster and keeps track so nobody gets asked twice. Try it on your next customer."
- **"Google already gives me a link."** "Right, and this uses that same link. The app is the part that makes you actually send it: one tap, a list, one reminder, and a sign for the counter."
- **"Is that allowed?"** "Yes. Google lets you ask your customers. What it bans is paying for reviews or only asking the happy ones. This asks everybody."
- **"I don't text customers."** "Then use the counter sign. They scan it with their camera while you ring them up."
- **"39 is too much."** "One new customer who picks you because of your reviews covers the year. And the first two weeks cost nothing."
- **"Send me something."** "I'll text you the app itself, it already has your name on it. Takes ten seconds to look at."

## After a yes

1. Ask them to open Google Business Profile, tap "Ask for reviews" and text you that link.
2. Send the file text from the setup page to Claude with "turn on Good Word for this shop".
3. Day 7: text "How many asks have you sent?" Day 14: ask for the first $39 by Venmo or Cash App.

## Never say

Never promise a number of reviews, a star rating or a spot on Google. Never offer to write or buy reviews. Never tell them to ask only happy customers.
