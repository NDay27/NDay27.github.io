    Questions

    Fair questions.

    Do I have to be on camera?

    No. Plenty of good reels are hands, tools, food and finished work. If you are up for it, ten seconds of the owner talking usually does best, and we will ask you two easy questions to get it.

    What is the free reel, exactly?

    We stop by for 15 minutes, film a few clips, and send you one finished reel the next day. It is yours to post whether or not you ever hire us. No card, no contract.

    What about my customers and staff?

    We ask before anyone is on camera and we do not film kids. If someone would rather not be in it, they are not in it.

    I do not have Instagram or TikTok.

    We can walk you through creating the pages in a few minutes. You log in; we never see your password. After that you add us as a helper on the page.

    How do I pay?

    Venmo or Cash App on the 1st of each month. You approve your first reels before the first payment.

    I am not near Morton.

    Clip Kit works anywhere. We send a short shot list every Monday, you film about ten minutes on your phone and text us the clips. We do the rest.
