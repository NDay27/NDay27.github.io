How to set up your Google Business Profile (free, step by step)

Your Google Business Profile is the box with your hours, reviews and call button that shows up when people search for you or "your service near me". It's free, and it's often the first thing a customer sees.

Before you start

  • A Google account you'll keep (use a business email if you have one)
  • Your exact business name, as it appears on your sign, truck or cards
  • Your phone number, hours and the towns you serve
  • 5 to 10 real photos: your work, your team, your vehicle or storefront

Step by step

  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Enter your business name. If Google finds an existing listing, claim it instead of making a new one.
  3. Pick your main category, like "Plumber" or "House cleaning service". It matters more than almost anything else, so pick the closest match.
  4. Add your location. If customers come to you, add your address. If you go to them, choose a service area and hide your home address.
  5. Add your phone number and website. No website yet? You can add one later.
  6. Verify. Google will offer one or more ways to confirm you're real, such as a short video, a phone or email code, or a postcard. Follow its steps; the options vary by business.
  7. Fill in everything else: hours, services with short descriptions, a plain description of what you do, and your photos.
  8. Get your review link from the profile ("Ask for reviews") and send it to happy customers after each job.

Mistakes that hurt

  • Stuffing keywords into your name ("Bob's Plumbing Best Plumber Peoria"). It breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.
  • Hours that are wrong, especially on holidays. Customers notice.
  • Buying or writing fake reviews. It's against Google's rules and against the law in the US. Just ask real customers.
  • No photos. Profiles with real photos look more trustworthy.
