How much does a small business website cost in 2026?

Short answer: anywhere from about $15 a month if you build it yourself to many thousands of dollars from an agency. Here's what you actually get at each level, so you can pick what fits.

The four usual options

OptionTypical costGood forWatch out for
Do it yourself (website builder)Roughly $15 to $50 a month for the plan, plus your timeOwners who enjoy tinkering and have evenings freeIt takes longer than expected, and many DIY sites never get finished
FreelancerOften $500 to $5,000 one timeA custom site with one person to talk toWho hosts it and makes changes after launch
AgencyOften $3,000 to $15,000 or moreLarger businesses with big content needsLong timelines and paying for meetings
Monthly website serviceOften $50 to $300 a monthOwners who want it handled with no big upfront billContract length and whether you keep the domain

Ranges are typical prices seen across the US market, not quotes. Always ask what's included.

Costs people forget

  • Domain name: usually $15 to $20 a year. Make sure it's registered in your name.
  • Hosting: sometimes free, sometimes $10 to $30 a month.
  • Changes after launch: ask how much a new photo or price update costs before you sign.
  • Your time: gathering photos, services and prices takes an hour or two whatever you choose.

Questions to ask any web designer

  1. Will I own the domain and the content?
  2. Can I see a preview or a sample before I pay?
  3. What does a small change cost after launch?
  4. Is there a contract, and how do I cancel?
  5. Will it work well on phones? (Ask to see one on your own phone.)
