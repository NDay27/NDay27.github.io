Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do I pay up front?

No. First we agree the scope in writing, then you see a free preview. You pay only after you approve the final version, by Venmo, Cash App or check.

Can I pay monthly instead?

Yes. The Starter Site is $0 down and $99 a month: a one-page site with tap-to-call, a Google listing fix-up, and hosting and small edits handled for you. Cancel any time. Or pay $300 once for the Essential site and own it outright.

What's included in the price?

Essential: up to 5 sections and 1 round of changes. Signature and Immersive: up to 7 sections and 2 rounds of changes. Prices are one-time, in USD. Hosting is free, and a domain, if you want one, costs the exact registrar price (usually $15–20 a year).

How long does it take?

Your free preview is usually ready the same day you ask. Once you say yes, a Starter Site or Essential site goes live the same day. Custom Signature and Immersive builds take longer, and I confirm that date in writing before you pay anything.

What do I need to send you?

To start, just your business name and town. For the preview: the services you want listed, how customers should reach you, and 3–5 photos if you have them. You never need to send a password.

I already have a Facebook page. Why a website?

Facebook works well for people who already follow you. A website is a page you own that can show up when someone searches for your service and town. Many owners keep both. I can't promise any ranking or number of customers.

Do you work with businesses outside Illinois?

Yes. I work with small businesses anywhere in the US, and with English-speaking businesses abroad, all by email. Prices are in USD, and local taxes and currency conversion fees aren't included.

Can you fix my current site instead?

Sometimes. I check first whether it's possible and what access it needs, then quote the specific repair. There's no flat price for repairs, and please never send passwords by email.

Who owns the site?

You own your content and your domain, and you can ask for the site files. Small text edits are free for 30 days after launch.

Will this bring more customers?

Nobody honest can promise that. A clear site removes the reasons people leave, like no call button, a broken layout, or no prices.

Is the Immersive sample a real place?

No. It's a sample built to show how a modeled 3D scene works on a site. It isn't a client.

What does "modeled" mean?

I build a stylized 3D scene from your photos or floor plan. It's an interpretation, not a measured replica. There's no on-site scanning, filming or exact dimensions. 360° photos are optional and supplied by you.
