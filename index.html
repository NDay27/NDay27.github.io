Questions owners ask

Do I pay up front?

No. First we agree the scope in writing, then you see a free preview. You pay only after you approve the final version in writing, through a payment link I send you.

What's included in the price?

Essential: up to 5 sections and 1 revision round. Signature and Immersive: up to 7 sections and 2 revision rounds. Prices are one-time, in USD. Hosting is free, and a domain, if you want one, costs the exact registrar price (usually $15–20 a year).

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly your details arrive, so I don't promise a date up front. Once we agree the scope and I have your content, I confirm a realistic preview and launch date in writing. Working toward a date or an event? Tell me in your first email.

What do I need to send you?

To start, just your business name and town. For the preview: the services you want listed, how customers should reach you (phone, email, hours), and 3–5 photos if you have them. I can start from what's already public about your business, and you never need to send a password.

Do you work with businesses outside Illinois?

Yes. I work with small businesses anywhere in the US, and with English-speaking businesses abroad, all by email. Prices are in USD, and local taxes and currency conversion fees aren't included.

How does it work if we never meet?

Everything happens by email. You tell me about your business, I send the scope in writing, and then I email you your free preview so you can look at it on your own phone. You reply with changes, approve the final version in writing, and only then get a payment link. No calls are needed, though you can always ask questions by email.

I already have a Facebook page. Why a website?

Facebook works well for people who already follow you. A website is a page you own that can show up when someone searches for your service and town, and it doesn't change when Facebook does. Many owners keep both: the site for new customers, Facebook for regulars. I can't promise any ranking or number of customers.

Can you fix my current site instead?

Sometimes. I check first whether it's possible and what access it needs, then quote the specific repair. There's no flat price for repairs, and please never send passwords by email.

Who owns the site?

You own your content and your domain, and you can ask for the site files. Small text edits are free for 30 days after launch.

Will this bring more customers?

Nobody honest can promise that. A clear site removes the reasons people leave, like no call button, a broken layout, or no prices.

Is the Immersive demo a real place?

No. It's a sample built to show how a 3D scene works on a site. It isn't a client.

What does "modeled" mean?

I build a stylized 3D scene from your photos or floor plan. It's an interpretation, not a measured replica. There's no on-site scanning, filming or exact dimensions.
