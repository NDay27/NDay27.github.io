Step 1

The drawing

Every patio starts as a plan: the circle, the fire pit and the seat wall, then marked out right on the yard.

Step 2

Dig it out

The sod and soft topsoil come out, so the patio sits on solid ground. R.W. Stoneworks does the dirt work too.

Step 3

A packed stone base

Crushed stone goes in and gets compacted. It's the part nobody sees, and the reason a patio stays flat.

Step 4

Stone and pavers

Flagstone fills the field, with paver bands around the fire pit and the outside edge.

Step 5

Fire pit and seat wall

Wall block goes up course by course: a ring for the fire in the middle and a curved wall to sit on around it.

Step 6

Caps, pillars and lights

Cap stones on top, pillars at the openings, steps up to the house and lights tucked under the caps.

Step 7

Light the fire

Then it's evening. The wall glows, the pillars glow, and the fire does the rest.

What we build

Six kinds of work. One crew.

From the excavation to the last cap stone, R.W. Stoneworks handles the whole job.

  • Patios & outdoor living

    Paver and flagstone patios, seat walls and lighting for the evenings.

  • Retaining walls

    Walls that hold a slope back and give the yard a level place to stand.

  • Fireplaces & fire pits

    Built-in fire pits and outdoor fireplaces in stone and block.

  • Stone & brick façades

    Decorative brick and stone work on the front of the house.

  • Custom paver designs

    Herringbone fields, contrast borders and bands laid out to fit the space.

  • Excavation & landscaping

    Digging, grading and the dirt work a project needs before the stone goes in.
