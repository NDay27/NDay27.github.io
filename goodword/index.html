What it does not do

It doesn't send texts by itself, and it can't promise a certain number of reviews or a spot on Google. It makes asking quick, so you actually do it.

Questions owners ask

Does it send the texts for me?

No, and that is on purpose. It writes the message and opens your own texting app with it ready. You press send, so the text comes from the number your customer already knows. There are no texting fees and no strange short-code numbers.

Is asking for reviews allowed?

Yes. Google lets a business ask its customers for reviews. What it does not allow is paying for them, trading discounts for them, or only asking the customers you think are happy. Good Word is built around asking everyone, the same way.

Where is my customer list kept?

On your phone only. The names and numbers you type are never uploaded to me or anyone else. You can save the list as a file or erase it whenever you like.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens from a link in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it sits next to your other apps.

What if I don't have a Google listing yet?

Then that is step one, and I can set it up with you. Reviews need a Google Business Profile to land on.

What happens if I cancel?

The app on your phone switches off at the end of the month. The QR codes you already printed keep working, and every review you earned stays on Google. They are yours.
