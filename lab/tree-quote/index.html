After a storm

A tree or limb on a power line? Stay back at least 30 feet, keep kids and pets away, and call Ameren Illinois or 911 before anyone touches it.
  1. Take photos of the damage from a safe spot. Your insurer will want them.
  2. Don't climb or cut a tree that's under tension or leaning on the house.
  3. Call us and say it's storm damage so the crew knows to come first.
  4. Ask the crew for a written quote you can send to your insurance.

Questions people ask

Does the quote cost anything?

No. The quote is free and you're never obligated to book.

Who actually does the work?

An independent local tree company. Peoria Tree Quote is a referral service. We don't cut trees ourselves, and we only send calls to companies with good reviews from local customers.

What affects the price?

Mostly the tree's size, how close it is to the house or lines, whether a bucket truck can reach it, and whether you want the wood hauled and the stump ground. The crew will price it after they see it.

Is winter a bad time for tree work?

No. Many crews like trimming in winter because the leaves are off and the ground is firm. It can also be easier to get on the schedule.

Can someone come out after a storm?

Call and say it's storm damage. If a tree is on a power line, call Ameren Illinois or 911 first.
