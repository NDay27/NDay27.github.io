03Straight about scope

What's included, and what isn't.

Included

  • Keeping your site online, and fixing it if it breaks
  • Small edits: hours, prices, a phone number, a photo swap, a holiday notice, one new service line
  • Reminders before your domain renews
  • On Get Found and Grow: posts on your Google listing, review reply drafts and a monthly report
  • On Grow: one section refreshed every quarter
  • Answers by text or email, from me

Not included

  • New pages or a redesign. Happy to do them, quoted in writing first
  • Your domain itself. You pay the registrar's price, usually $15 to $20 a year
  • Ad spend, social media accounts or email accounts
  • Writing or buying reviews. I only draft replies to real ones, and you approve every reply
  • Promises about Google rankings, calls or sales. Nobody honest can promise those
  • New photography. You send the photos, I make them look good

05Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need a care plan?

No. Your site works without one, and free hosting stays free on the one-time plans. Small text edits are free for the first 30 days after launch. After that, without a plan, each change is quoted before I do it.

Why a 3-month minimum?

Google posts and review replies take a few months to be worth anything, and the first month includes setting things up. After 3 months it's month to month, and you can stop with a text.

What counts as a small edit?

Anything that changes words or photos on a page you already have: hours, prices, a phone number, a photo swap, a holiday notice, one new service line. A new page or a new section is bigger, and I quote it first.

Will this get me to the top of Google?

I can't promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What the plans do is keep your listing complete, active and answered, which is the part you control. The monthly report shows Google's own numbers so you can judge it yourself.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. You add me as a manager on your Business Profile, which takes about a minute. You stay the owner, and you can remove me any time. Step by step here.

Who posts the review replies?

I write a draft for each new review and send them to you. Nothing is posted until you say it's good. I never write reviews, and I never ask for them in exchange for anything.

I'm on the $0 down, $99 a month Starter plan. Do I need this?

Keep It Running is already part of what you have: hosting and changes whenever you ask. If you want the Google profile work too, ask me and I'll tell you exactly what it would add before anything changes.

Can I switch or stop?

Switch plans any month. After the first 3 months, stop with one text. Your site stays up, it stays yours, and you can ask for the files any time.

How do I pay?

Venmo, Cash App or a check, once a month on the same date. I send a request for the exact amount, so you just tap approve.

Is this for sites you didn't build?

Usually yes, if I can get access to make changes. Send me the address and I'll tell you honestly whether it fits a plan.

Spend the month on the work. I'll handle the site.
