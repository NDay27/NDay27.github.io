What a manager can and can't do

Managers can edit your details, add photos and posts, and reply to reviews. They can't remove owners, add other people or delete the profile. That stays with you.

Can't find your listing?

It may be under a different Google account, or someone else may control it. Tell me what you see. Sorting it out is the $149 Google profile fix-up, only if you want it.

    07Quick answers

    Before you ask.

    I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

    No. Phone photos of real jobs beat stock photos every time, and I crop and brighten them. If you have none yet, I use clean illustrations until you do.

    Do I pay anything before it's live?

    No. The only thing that could come first is a domain, if you want one and buy it yourself, at the registrar's price.

    What if I forget something on the list?

    Send what you have. I fill the gaps with what's on your preview and your Google listing, and you check everything before it goes live.

    Can I change things after it's live?

    Yes. Small text edits are free for 30 days after launch. After that, changes are quoted first, or covered by a care plan if you pick one.
