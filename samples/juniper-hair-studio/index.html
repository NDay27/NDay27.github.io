Every appointment

Four movements, never rushed.

  1. I

    The consult

    Five quiet minutes in the chair before anything gets wet. What you want, what you don't, and how much time you give your hair each morning.

  2. II

    The wash

    A warm rinse, a slow scalp massage and products picked for your hair, not the shelf.

  3. III

    The work

    The cut or the color, done carefully, with a mirror check before anything is final.

  4. IV

    The finish

    Styled, then shown how to do it yourself at home with the tools you already own.

Questions

Asked often, answered here.

Do you take walk-ins?

When a chair is open, yes. Call ahead and we'll tell you honestly how long the wait is.

Highlights or balayage: which one do I want?

Highlights are placed in foils for brighter, more even lightness. Balayage is painted by hand for a softer grow-out. Book the free consult and we'll look at your hair together.

Do I need a consult before color?

For a big change, yes. It's free, takes about fifteen minutes and saves surprises on the day.

What if I need to cancel?

Every salon sets its own cancellation policy. A real site states it here in one clear sentence.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any age. Mornings are usually calmest for little ones.
