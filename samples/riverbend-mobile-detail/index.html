Paint correction

Those spiderwebs come out.

Questions

Before you book.

Something else? Call or text (309) 555-0167.

Do you need my water or power?

No. The van carries its own water tank and a quiet generator, so it works in an apartment lot or an office parking lot too.

Do I need to be there?

Only at the start and the end. Leave the car unlocked or hand over the keys, then we'll text you for the walkaround.

What if it rains?

Interiors can go ahead in light rain. For exterior work we move you to the next open slot and text you the new time.

How long does ceramic coating last?

The coating in our correction package is rated for about two years with normal hand washing. It needs a day to cure, so plan to keep the car dry overnight.

Can you get dog hair out?

Yes, that's the pet hair add-on. Rubber brushes and a strong vacuum get it out of the carpet weave, not just off the top.

How do I pay?

After the walkaround, by card, cash or a payment app. Nothing is charged up front.
