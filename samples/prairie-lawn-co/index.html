Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the gate unlocked or send us the code, and keep pets inside on your day. You'll get a photo text when we're done.

What if it rains on my day?

Mowing wet grass tears it and ruts the yard, so the route slides a day and we text you the new day that morning.

Do you bag the clippings?

We mulch them back in, which feeds the lawn. If you'd rather have them bagged, say so and it happens every visit.

Is there a contract?

No. Mowing is week to week. Skip a week for a party, or stop for the season, with one text.

How do I pay?

One bill at the end of each month. Card, check or a payment app, whichever is easiest for you.

When do I sign up for snow?

By mid November. Snow routes fill up, and drives already on the list get cleared first after a storm.
