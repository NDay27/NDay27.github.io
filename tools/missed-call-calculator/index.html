What are missed calls costing your business?

Most owners know they miss calls while they're on a job. Few have put a number on it. Enter your own numbers below; nothing is sent anywhere.

This is a rough estimate from the numbers you entered (a month counted as 4.33 weeks), not a promise of what any service will win back.

What to do about it

  1. Look at your call log for last week and count the missed calls you never returned. That's your real first number.
  2. Turn on voicemail and a text-back if your phone plan has one, so callers know you'll get back to them.
  3. Call back fast. People who can't reach you often call the next business on the list.
  4. Or let Phone Desk answer. My AI receptionist picks up calls you forward to it, takes the name, number, address and job, and sends you the summary.
